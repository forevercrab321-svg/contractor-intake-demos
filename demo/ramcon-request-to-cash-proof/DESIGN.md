# RAMCON Request-to-Cash Proof — Visual Contract

## Direction

Industrial operations control tower: quiet dark navy surfaces, field-safety orange for action, amber/red only for exceptions, and compact labels for auditability. The interface should feel like an operational proof rather than a replacement marketing website.

## Truth rules

- Always display `PRIVATE CONCEPT`, `SYNTHETIC DATA`, and `NO LIVE INTEGRATION` above the fold.
- Never use RAMCON logo files, photography, testimonials, customer data, financial outcomes, or claims of affiliation.
- Treat every owner, site, amount, age, queue and outcome as illustrative.
- Human approval must remain visible at every handoff.

## Tokens

- Background: `#071521`
- Surface: `#0a2235` / `#102d43`
- Action: `#ff6a2a`
- Review: `#ffb73c`
- Exception: `#ff5a4f`
- Confirmation: `#a8de75`
- Border: `#244459`
- Radius: 12–22px

## Responsive rule

Desktop emphasizes the two-column intake/control view. At 560px, all interaction becomes one column, exception filters scroll horizontally, and the stage line becomes a vertical checklist. No hover-only information.
